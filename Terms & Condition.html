Bookings & Reservations 02

All bookings for ski packages, accommodation, and guided activities must be made in advance. Reservations are only confirmed upon receipt of written or electronic confirmation from our reservations team.

  • A 30% deposit is required to confirm all multi-day packages and cabin bookings.
  • Full payment is due 14 days before your arrival date.
  • Group bookings of 10 or more require a separate group contract.
  • We reserve the right to decline or cancel bookings where safety criteria cannot be met.

Cancellations & Refunds 03

Our cancellation policy is as follows:

  • 30+ days before arrival: Full refund minus a 5% processing fee.
  • 15–29 days before arrival: 50% refund of total booking value.
  • 7–14 days before arrival: 25% refund.
  • Less than 7 days: No refund. Travel insurance is strongly recommended.

Alpine Adventures reserves the right to cancel bookings due to extreme weather, trail closures, or safety concerns. In such cases, a full refund or complimentary rescheduling will be offered.

Safety & Liability 04

All guests participate in alpine activities at their own risk. Alpine Adventures takes every precaution to maintain a safe environment but cannot eliminate the inherent risks of winter sports.

  • Guests must complete and sign a waiver of liability before participating in guided activities.
  • Our guides are fully certified and hold current first aid qualifications.
  • Guests must follow all trail markings, signage, and instructions from resort staff.
  • Alpine Adventures is not liable for injury, illness, or loss resulting from failure to follow safety guidelines.

Guest Conduct 05

We expect all guests to behave in a manner that is respectful of other guests, staff, and the mountain environment.

  • Guests found to be intoxicated on the slopes will be immediately removed and may be banned from the resort.
  • Skiing or snowboarding outside designated areas without a certified guide is prohibited.
  • Any wilful damage to resort property will be charged in full to the responsible guest.

Payments & Pricing 06

  • Prices are subject to change without notice. Confirmed bookings are price-protected.
  • Resort gift cards are valid for 24 months from purchase and are non-refundable.
  • Season passes are non-transferable and non-refundable after activation.
