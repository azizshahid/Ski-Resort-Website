Who We Are

A Resort Built by Mountain People

Alpine Adventures was founded in 2005 by a group of professional skiers and outdoor enthusiasts who believed that the mountain experience should be extraordinary for every guest — not just the experts.

From 50 pristine ski trails to luxury cabins and world-class dining, we've built a resort that honours the raw beauty of winter whilst wrapping it in the warmth of genuine hospitality.

Our Mission

Adventure for Everyone

We believe that the mountain doesn't care how experienced you are — it has something to offer everyone. That's why we invest in beginner-friendly trails alongside expert runs, kids' programmes alongside extreme sports, and quiet spa retreats alongside adrenaline-fuelled adventures.

Our mission is simple: create unforgettable winter memories for every guest, every time.

What Drives Us

Our Values

Four principles guide every decision we make — from trail grooming at 5 AM to the final goodnight from our concierge.

Safety First

Every trail, activity, and piece of equipment is maintained to the highest safety standards. No exceptions.

Pure Adventure

We preserve the raw, thrilling essence of mountain sports while making them accessible to all skill levels.

Environmental Care

We are committed to sustainable operations that protect the mountain ecosystems we depend on.

Community

We support local guides, businesses, and communities — because the mountain belongs to everyone.
